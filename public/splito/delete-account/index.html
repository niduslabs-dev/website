Delete your Splito account

Splito: Split & Track Expenses, by Nidus Labs

How to ask

Email support@niduslabs.dev from the email address you sign in to Splito with, with the subject “Delete my Splito account”. If you can't use that address, say which one you signed in with and you'll be asked to confirm it.

Your account is deleted within 30 days, and you'll get an email when it's done. Deleting from inside the app, under Account, is coming soon.

What's deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address and phone number
  • Your Google and Apple sign-ins, and your passkeys
  • Your UPI ID
  • Your signed-in devices, sessions and notification tokens
  • Your personal expenses, which only you could see
  • Your friends list, blocks and notification settings

What's kept, and why

Expenses and payments you shared with other people stay in their records, because they're part of those people's balances too. They'll show you by name only, with no link to an account, email address or phone number.

Server logs are deleted after a few days and crash reports after 30 days. Neither is kept longer because of your account.

Removing just some data

You don't have to delete your account to remove things. In Splito you can edit your details under Account, and delete any expense or payment you're part of. To remove anything else, or to ask for a copy of your data, email support@niduslabs.dev.

If someone added you

If a friend added your name and number to Splito but you've never used it, email support@niduslabs.dev with the number and it will be removed.
