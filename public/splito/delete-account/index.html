Delete your Splito account

Splito: Split & Track Expenses, by Nidus Labs

In the app

Open Splito and go to Account › Delete account. You'll see what's deleted and what's kept, and you confirm by holding the button. It happens straight away.

You need to be settled up with everyone first: every friend and every person you added by phone number, in every group and every currency. If anything is still open, the app lists it, and tapping a balance takes you to the person or group to settle it. People you added by name only don't count; they're deleted with you.

By email

If you can't use the app, email support@niduslabs.dev from the email address you sign in to Splito with, with the subject “Delete my Splito account”. If you can't use that address, say which one you signed in with and you'll be asked to confirm it. You'll still need to be settled up with everyone; you'll be told what's open if you aren't. Your account is deleted within 30 days, and you'll get an email when it's done.

What's deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address and phone number
  • Your Google and Apple sign-ins, and your passkeys; if you signed in with Apple, Splito's access to your Apple ID is revoked too
  • Your UPI ID
  • Your signed-in devices, sessions and notification tokens
  • Your personal expenses, which only you could see, and the people you added by name only
  • Your friends list, blocks, notification settings and activity
  • Your place in every group

What's kept, and why

Expenses and payments you shared with other people stay in their records, because they're part of their history too. You were settled up, so no balance is left. They show you as “Deleted account” (or the name a friend saved for you), with no link to an account, email address or phone number. Their amounts can't be changed or deleted any more.

If you sign up again later, even with the same Google or Apple account or phone number, you start a new account with no history. It's never joined to the old one.

Server logs are deleted after a few days and crash reports after 30 days. Neither is kept longer because of your account.

Removing just some data

You don't have to delete your account to remove things. In Splito you can edit your details under Account, and delete any expense or payment you're part of. To remove anything else, or to ask for a copy of your data, email support@niduslabs.dev.

If someone added you

If a friend added your name and number to Splito but you've never used it, email support@niduslabs.dev with the number and it will be removed.
